# Systematic review

Write the question, inclusion and exclusion rules, search facets, sources, and
date before screening. Deduplicate by DOI, PMID, arXiv ID, or normalized
title/year. Track included, excluded, and unresolved records with reasons.
Separate peer-reviewed work, preprints, and secondary sources. Report search
limits and uncertainty; do not imply exhaustive coverage unless it was tested.
