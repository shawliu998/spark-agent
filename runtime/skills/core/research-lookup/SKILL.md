# Research lookup

Decompose the question into two or more useful facets. Search the most relevant
available sources, such as OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed/Europe PMC, or arXiv.
Keep raw results separate from claims, record the search date, and preserve
source identity. Treat snippets as discovery leads, not evidence. Stop when
new results add no material themes or when the declared search budget is met.
